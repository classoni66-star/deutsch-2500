How to study

Look at the visual, listen to the German pronunciation, then say the word aloud before reading the example sentence. Read the word-by-word IPA under the sentence to check each sound.

Pronunciation tip
Listen first, then repeat slowly. The soft ç in ich, nicht, wichtig is made at the front of the mouth — like a whispered "y" — not like the "ch" in English "cheese".
