How to say it

Read the blue line under each word, then tap the audio button. CAPITALS show the stressed syllable.

khSOFT ch — after i, e, ä, ö, ü. Whisper a "y" (ich, nicht, sprechen).
KHTHROATY ch — after a, o, u. Like Scottish "loch" (Buch, machen, nach).
ÖSay "eh" with rounded lips (schön, können).
ÜSay "ee" with rounded lips (müssen, für).
